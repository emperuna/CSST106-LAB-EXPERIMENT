## Concepts

- An edge is a sharp change in intensity. A derivative measures that change, but also amplifies noise, so the image is blurred first.
- **Sobel** estimates the gradient in x and y. Their combined magnitude is a graded map of edge strength.
- **Canny** continues from the gradient: it thins edges to one pixel with non-maximum suppression, then uses two thresholds. Strong edges are kept, and weak edges survive only if connected to a strong one.

## Procedure

1. Convert the image to grayscale.
2. Apply a Gaussian blur.
3. Compute the Sobel x and y gradients with `cv2.Sobel`, then combine them with `cv2.magnitude`.
4. Run `cv2.Canny` with low and high thresholds.
5. Compare the original, Sobel and Canny results side by side.
